Step-by-Step Mixing & Mastering Roadmap

Click any step below to expand detailed pro tips, FL Studio native plugin choices, and common mistakes to avoid.

Gain staging is the foundation of a clean mix. If your faders are maxed out before you even touch a plugin, your plugins (especially emulated compressors and saturators) will clip internally and sound harsh.

FL Studio Pro Tip

Use the channel volume knob or clip gain inside the Playlist audio clips to turn down loud samples before they hit the mixer insert. Use Fruity Peak Meter or Wave Candy to monitor true levels.

Common Mistake

Pushing the Master fader down to fix clipping. Fix your mix balance in the multi-track mixer instead; keep the Master fader at 0.0dB until the final limiting stage.

Every instrument needs its own frequency home. Sub-bass owns 20Hz-60Hz, kick punch around 80Hz-120Hz, vocals shine in 2kHz-5kHz. High-pass filter (HPF) non-bass instruments below 30Hz-80Hz to remove inaudible rumble.

FL Studio Pro Tip

Use Parametric EQ 2 in Linear Phase mode or High quality setting. Right-click bands to change filter types (e.g., 40dB/oct slope for clean low cuts). Use the spectral analyzer view to spot resonant frequencies.

Common Mistake

Over-equalizing in solo mode. Always mix in context with the rest of the beat so you don't carve away essential body.

Compression reduces dynamic range by turning down loud transients. Use slower attack times (20ms-30ms) on drums to let the initial punch through, and faster release times for rhythmic pumping control.

FL Studio Pro Tip

Try parallel compression: route your drum bus to a send track, crush it heavily with Fruity Compressor or Maximus (using aggressive downward compression), and blend it quietly under the dry signal for thickness.

Common Mistake

Over-compressing every single insert. If an instrument already has stable volume, it doesn't need compression.

Digital audio can sometimes sound sterile. Saturation introduces pleasant harmonic distortion that thickens the sound and tricks the human ear into perceiving higher loudness without increasing peak levels.

FL Studio Pro Tip

Use WaveShaper for custom distortion curves on 808s and synths. Keep the curve slightly soft-clipped near the top edges for warm tube character. Fruity Waveshaper or Hardcore distorsion modules work wonders.

Common Mistake

Adding heavy distortion on the master bus without monitoring inter-sample peaks. Keep saturation subtle on instrument busses.

Never put heavy reverb directly as an insert on every instrument (unless going for a special Lo-Fi aesthetic). Instead, route multiple instruments to a dedicated Reverb Send track to unify your mix space.

FL Studio Pro Tip

Place an EQ *after* your reverb plugin on your send track and high-pass the reverb at 300Hz and low-pass at 8kHz. This keeps your low-end clean and prevents muddy reverb wash.

Common Mistake

Putting delays and reverbs on low-end elements like Kicks and 808s, which smears the stereo image and destroys punch.

Mastering is the final polish. Use subtle bus EQ, multi-band compression, and a transparent brickwall limiter to achieve competitive streaming loudness (-14 to -9 LUFS) without crushing transients.

FL Studio Pro Tip

Master with FL Studio's powerhouse plugin Maximus. Use the default mastering presets or gently compress 3 bands with a Ceiling set to -1.0 dB True Peak to prevent clipping on Spotify or Apple Music.

Common Mistake

Slamming the limiter threshold down by -10dB. If your mix lacks energy, fix the balance in the mix rather than destroying it with a heavy limiter.

Native FL Studio Plugin Cheat Sheet

You don't need expensive VSTs to make a Billboard hit. Filter FL Studio's powerful stock plugins below.

EQ & Filters Stock Essential

Fruity Parametric EQ 2

A high-precision 7-band parametric equalizer with zero latency, low CPU usage, and crystal clear visual spectral analyzer.

Pro Settings Guide:

Set quality to High (16x) for mastering. Use band 1 as 40dB/oct High-Pass filter and band 7 as Low-Pass filter.

Dynamics Stock Essential

Maximus

A multi-band compressor, limiter, and expander powerhouse. Great for glue compression and final master limiting.

Pro Settings Guide:

Use preset Master RMS or Clear Master. Keep ceiling at -1.0 dB for streaming platforms.

Saturation Stock Essential

WaveShaper

A wave-shaping distortion tool that lets you draw custom input/output transfer curves for warm saturation and grit.

Pro Settings Guide:

Bend the center line slightly upward into an 'S' curve to add subtle tube warmth to 808 sub bass.

Spatial & Time Stock Essential

Fruity Reverb 2

A lush algorithmic reverb featuring built-in low cut, high cut, and early reflection controls for creating deep mix spaces.

Pro Settings Guide:

Put on a Send track. Turn up Low Cut knob to 350Hz to keep sub frequencies clean.

Analysis Stock Essential

Wave Candy

A flexible visualization tool including spectrum analyzer, oscilloscope, stereo meter, and peak meter.

Pro Settings Guide:

Switch mode to Vectorscope to check phase correlation and stereo width issues instantly.

Dynamics Stock Essential

Fruity Limiter

A dual-function plugin featuring a compressor on the left and a noise gate & transparent brickwall limiter on the right.

Pro Settings Guide:

Set ceiling to -1.0dB and adjust gain until you get 2-4dB of gain reduction on drops.
